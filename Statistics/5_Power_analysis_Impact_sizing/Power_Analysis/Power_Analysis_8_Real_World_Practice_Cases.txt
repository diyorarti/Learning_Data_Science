POWER ANALYSIS — 8 REAL-WORLD DATA SCIENTIST PRACTICE CASES
===========================================================

You are a Junior Data Scientist at a technology company. Product, Marketing,
Operations, and Analytics teams ask you to plan studies before collecting data.
Your responsibility is to determine the sample size needed for each study and
communicate a justified recommendation.

These assignments intentionally do not include solutions, formulas, or code.
Choose the correct statistical method and solve each case yourself.


CASE 1 — ONE POPULATION MEAN 
Company: QuickCart | Department: Operations Analytics
Topic: Delivery Time Performance

MESSAGE FROM THE OPERATIONS MANAGER:
"Our platform promises customers an average delivery time of 30 minutes.
We recently upgraded our routing system, and we want to check whether our
average delivery time has changed.

We expect the new average could be around 27 minutes, and our historical
delivery-time standard deviation is approximately 8 minutes.

We want 90% statistical power and a 5% significance level. How many completed
deliveries should we analyze before deciding whether the average has changed?"

YOUR ASSIGNMENT:
- Identify the appropriate statistical test.
- State the null and alternative hypotheses.
- Calculate the minimum detectable difference.
- Estimate the required sample size with Python.
- Explain your recommendation to the Operations Manager.

PLANNING ASSUMPTIONS:
Two-sided test; independent delivery times; approximately normally
distributed observations.


CASE 2 — TWO INDEPENDENT POPULATIO N MEANS
Company: ShopNova | Department: Product Experimentation
Topic: Recommendation Algorithm A/B Test

MESSAGE FROM THE PRODUCT MANAGER:
"Our current recommendation algorithm generates an average order value of
120 PLN. The Machine Learning team has developed a new algorithm that we
believe could increase average order value to 128 PLN.

Historical data indicates a standard deviation of approximately 35 PLN.
We want to run a randomized A/B test with equal traffic allocation, 80%
statistical power, and a 5% significance level.

How many customers should we assign to each group?"

YOUR ASSIGNMENT:
- Identify the statistical test and write the hypotheses.
- Calculate Cohen's d.
- Calculate required sample size per group and total sample size with Python.
- Interpret the result as an experiment-planning recommendation.

PLANNING ASSUMPTIONS:
Two-sided test; independent observations; normal outcomes; equal population
variances; 50/50 allocation. The metric is spending per randomized customer
over a fixed observation window.s


CASE 3 — TWO DEPENDENT POPULATION MEANS
Company: HelpFlow | Department: Customer Experience
Topic: AI Assistant and Customer Support Response Time

MESSAGE FROM THE CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE DIRECTOR:
"We're introducing an AI-powered assistant to help our customer support
agents respond faster.

We want to measure each agent's average response time before and after
adopting the assistant. Our goal is to detect a reduction of at least
2 minutes. Based on our pilot study, the standard deviation of the
before-and-after differences is approximately 5 minutes.

We need 80% statistical power with a significance level of 0.05.
How many support agents should participate in the study?"

YOUR ASSIGNMENT:
- Identify the appropriate paired statistical test.
- Define the paired-difference variable and hypotheses.
- Calculate the required number of agents with Python.
- Explain why the SD of paired differences matters.

PLANNING ASSUMPTIONS:
One-sided test for faster responses; paired differences are approximately
normally distributed and independent across agents; each agent provides
one before-and-after pair.


CASE 4 — THREE OR MORE INDEPENDENT POPULATION MEANS (ANOVA)
Company: LearnSphere | Department: Growth & Product
Topic: Comparing Three Onboarding Experiences

MESSAGE FROM THE HEAD OF GROWTH:
"We have developed three onboarding experiences for our online learning
platform. We want to understand whether onboarding design affects the
average number of minutes new users spend learning during their first week.

The Product team expects these group averages:
  Experience A: 40 minutes
  Experience B: 45 minutes
  Experience C: 52 minutes

Based on previous cohorts, the common within-group standard deviation is
around 15 minutes. We need an experiment with 80% power and alpha = 0.05.

How many users should we recruit for each onboarding experience?"

YOUR ASSIGNMENT:
- Identify the correct statistical test and write the hypotheses.
- Calculate Cohen's f using the expected group means and SD.
- Determine required sample size per group and overall using Python.
- Explain what an overall significant test would and would not establish.

PLANNING ASSUMPTIONS:
Three independent, equally sized groups; normally distributed outcomes;
equal within-group population variances. The primary objective is the
overall ANOVA, not individual pairwise comparisons.

Following recent infrastructure improvements, we believe the failure

We want to verify whether the failure rate is now lower than our historical

How many transactions should we analyze?"

YOUR ASSIGNMENT:
- Identify the test and specify null and alternative proportions.
- Calculate the absolute and relative MDE.
- Estimate required sample size using Python.

CASE 6 — TWO INDEPENDENT POPULATION PROPORTIONS
Company: MarketPilot | Department: Conversion Optimization
Topic: Checkout Conversion A/B Test

MESSAGE FROM THE CONVERSION OPTIMIZATION MANAGER:
"Our current checkout process has a conversion rate of approximately 10%.
The design team has created a simplified checkout experience. We believe
it could increase conversions to 12%.

We want to test this change before releasing it to all users.
The experiment must have 80% statistical power and alpha = 0.05.

However, we're concerned about exposing too many users to an untested
design. Please compare a 50/50 traffic allocation with an 80/20 allocation,
where 80% of users remain in the control group.

How many users do we need under each design?"

YOUR ASSIGNMENT:
- Identify the statistical test and state the hypotheses.
- Calculate the absolute and relative MDE.
- Determine control, treatment, and total samples for both allocations.
- Recommend a design, balancing statistical efficiency and rollout risk.

PLANNING ASSUMPTIONS:
Two-sided test; independent users; one binary conversion outcome per user;
no overlap between randomized groups.


CASE 7 — CATEGORICAL GOODNESS-OF-FIT
Company: StreamWave | Department: Marketing Analytics
Topic: Subscription Plan Preferences

MESSAGE FROM THE MARKETING DIRECTOR:
"Our platform offers three subscription plans: Basic, Standard, and Premium.

Historically, customers have selected the plans in these proportions:
  Basic:    50%
  Standard: 30%
  Premium:  20%

Following a pricing update, we expect customer preferences to shift:
  Basic:    40%
  Standard: 35%
  Premium:  25%

We want to determine whether the overall distribution of subscription
choices differs from the historical distribution.

We need 80% power and alpha = 0.05. How many new customer subscriptions
should we observe?"

YOUR ASSIGNMENT:
- Identify the statistical test.
- Define the null and alternative category distributions.
- Calculate Cohen's w and degrees of freedom.
- Solve for the required total sample size with Python.
- Check expected category counts and explain your recommendation.

PLANNING ASSUMPTIONS:
Independent subscription selections; one observation per new customer;
fixed historical proportions.


CASE 8 — CATEGORICAL INDEPENDENCE
Company: CloudBox | Department: Customer Analytics
Topic: Device Type and Premium Subscription Behavior

MESSAGE FROM THE SENIOR DATA SCIENTIST:
"We suspect that users' device types are associated with whether they
purchase a premium subscription.

Approximately 60% of our users primarily access CloudBox through mobile
devices, while 40% use desktop.

Based on preliminary research, we expect:
  Mobile users:  15% purchase a premium subscription.
  Desktop users: 25% purchase a premium subscription.

Before collecting a new dataset, we want to ensure we have enough
observations to detect an association between device type and purchasing
behavior.

Use 90% statistical power and alpha = 0.05.
How many users should we include in the study?"

YOUR ASSIGNMENT:
- Identify the statistical test and formulate hypotheses.
- Construct the expected 2 x 2 joint probability table.
- Calculate expected cell probabilities under independence.
- Calculate Cohen's w and the degrees of freedom.
- Determine required total sample size using Python.
- Explain the result without implying that association proves causation.

PLANNING ASSUMPTIONS:
Independent users; device group proportions treated as fixed in planning.
