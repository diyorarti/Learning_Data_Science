T-TEST PRACTICE PACK

Use alpha = 0.05 unless you intentionally want to practice another significance level.

1) ONE-SAMPLE T-TEST
    File: 01_one_sample_t_mean.csv

    Scenario:
    A delivery company claims that its average delivery time is 30 minutes.
    The population standard deviation is UNKNOWN.

    Research question:
    Is the true average delivery time different from 30 minutes?

    Your tasks:
    - Identify the correct t-test.
    - Decide whether the test is one-tailed or two-tailed.
    - Write H0 and Ha.
    - Calculate n, sample mean, and sample standard deviation.
    - Calculate standard error.
    - Calculate the observed t-statistic.
    - Calculate degrees of freedom.
    - Find the critical t-value(s).
    - Make a decision using critical values.
    - Calculate the p-value.
    - Make a decision using the p-value.
    - Write the final statistical conclusion.


2) INDEPENDENT TWO-SAMPLE T-TEST
    File: 02_independent_two_sample_t.csv

    Scenario:
    A company compares employee productivity scores between two independent teams:
    Group A and Group B.
    The population standard deviations are UNKNOWN.

    Research question:
    Are the population mean productivity scores different between Group A and Group B?

    Your tasks:
    - Identify the correct t-test.
    - Decide whether the test is one-tailed or two-tailed.
    - Write H0 and Ha.
    - Separate Group A and Group B.
    - Calculate sample size, sample mean, and sample standard deviation for each group.
    - Use an independent two-sample t-test. Welch's t-test is recommended.
    - Calculate the observed t-statistic.
    - Calculate/obtain the appropriate degrees of freedom.
    - Make a decision using the p-value.
    - Write the final statistical conclusion.


3) PAIRED T-TEST
    File: 03_paired_t.csv

    Scenario:
    The same participants take a skills test before and after a training program.

    Research question:
    Has the training increased the average score?

    Important:
    Define each paired difference as:
    d = score_after - score_before

    Your tasks:
    - Identify the correct t-test.
    - Decide whether the test is one-tailed or two-tailed.
    - Write H0 and Ha in terms of the population mean difference.
    - Calculate each participant's difference.
    - Calculate the mean difference and standard deviation of the differences.
    - Calculate the standard error of the mean difference.
    - Calculate the observed t-statistic.
    - Calculate degrees of freedom.
    - Find the appropriate critical t-value.
    - Make a decision using critical values.
    - Calculate the p-value.
    - Make a decision using the p-value.
    - Write the final statistical conclusion.
