ONE-WAY ANOVA PRACTICE

File:
01_one_way_anova.csv

Scenario:
A company wants to compare employee performance scores after three different
training programs: A, B, and C.

Research question:
Are the population mean performance scores the same across all three training programs?

Use:
alpha = 0.05

Your tasks:
1. Identify why One-way ANOVA is appropriate.
2. Write H0 and Ha.
3. Separate the three independent groups.
4. Calculate each group:
   - sample size
   - sample mean
5. Calculate the grand mean.
6. Calculate SS_between.
7. Calculate SS_within.
8. Calculate degrees of freedom:
   - df_between
   - df_within
9. Calculate:
   - MS_between
   - MS_within
10. Calculate the F-statistic.
11. Find the critical F-value.
12. Make a decision using the critical-value method.
13. Calculate the p-value.
14. Make a decision using the p-value.
15. Write the final statistical conclusion.
16. If H0 is rejected, remember that ANOVA only tells you that at least one
    population mean differs; it does not tell you which groups differ.

Do not open answer_key.txt until you finish.
